## Why I Built It

I wanted a place where I could showcase my projects, share what I learn, and create an online portfolio that represents me better than a traditional CV.

## Technologies

This portfolio is built with:

- Next.js
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS

While customizing the template, I learned more about responsive design, reusable components, and modern frontend development.

## Final Thoughts

Building this website was more than creating a portfolio. It was also an opportunity to improve my frontend skills and organize my projects in one place.
