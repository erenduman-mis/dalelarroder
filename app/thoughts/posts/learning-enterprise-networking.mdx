## Getting Started

Networking became one of the subjects I enjoyed the most during my studies. At first, concepts like VLANs, routing, and switching seemed complicated, but practicing with real projects made them much easier to understand.

## What I Learned

Some of the topics I worked on include:

- Cisco Packet Tracer
- VLAN configuration
- Router-on-a-stick
- Access Control Lists (ACL)
- Basic firewall concepts

My internship also helped me understand how these technologies are used in real business environments.

## Looking Ahead

I plan to continue improving my networking skills by learning more about enterprise infrastructure, firewalls, virtualization, and cloud technologies.
