## Introduction

My internship allowed me to experience how an IT department works in a real company. It was my first opportunity to apply what I had learned at university in a professional environment.

## What I Worked On

During the internship, I gained hands-on experience with:

- Windows Server
- Active Directory
- Linux
- VMware
- Enterprise networking
- Basic firewall management

I also learned how important communication, documentation, and teamwork are in daily IT operations.

## Final Thoughts

This experience helped me discover the areas of IT that interest me the most. It also motivated me to continue learning system administration, networking, and cloud technologies.
